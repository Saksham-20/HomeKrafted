# Image licences — sourced stock photography

Provenance for every committed image that was not shot for Homekrafted
and is not an owner-supplied asset. All entries below are **real
photographs** downloaded from Pexels under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) — free for commercial
use, no attribution owed, modification allowed. None is AI-generated
(checked against the platform's AI labels at download time).

**These are stand-ins for the demo catalogue.** A stock photograph is a
real photo of a generic dish or object, not the listed maker's actual
product — acceptable for seeded demo storefronts (owner decision,
2026-08-31, M56), and replaced listing-by-listing as real kitchens and
makers upload their own photography. New entries are processed by
`client/scripts/process-stock-images.mjs` (1000×1000 JPEG q80, EXIF
stripped — the M25 privacy rule).

Category tiles under `categories/` that share a name with a row below
are crops of the same file (`sweets-ladoos` ← besan-ladoo, an existing
owner asset; `candles-home` ← sandalwood-soy-candle; `handmade-jewellery`
← oxidised-silver-jhumkas; `art-prints` ← hand-painted-botanical-print;
`personalised-gifts` ← personalised-name-keyring).
